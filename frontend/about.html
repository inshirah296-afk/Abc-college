Our Story

A campus built around participation

ABC College is a fictional institution created for this student mini-project, imagined as a mid-sized college with a strong culture of student-run clubs, technical societies and inter-department competitions.

The Event Management Portal was built to solve a very real campus problem: event information scattered across notice boards, WhatsApp groups and word of mouth. Here, every technical fest, cultural night, workshop and sports fixture lives in one searchable, always up-to-date place.

What ABC College stands for
  • Every student gets equal visibility into campus opportunities.
  • Clubs and departments run their events without administrative friction.
  • Feedback closes the loop, so events improve year on year.
